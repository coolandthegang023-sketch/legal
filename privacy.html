Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Content Studio ("the App") handles data. The App runs on the owner's own computer.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile of the connected account (e.g. TikTok open ID, username, avatar) — to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by the platform — to upload videos you approve.
  • Statistics of videos the App posted (views, likes, comments) — to show performance reports to the owner.

2. How it is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-GCM) on the owner's computer only. Nothing is stored on third-party servers by us.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (e.g. TikTok's API when uploading to TikTok).

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. Removing the key in the App deletes the stored tokens; you can also revoke access in the platform's settings at any time. To ask for deletion, email coolandthegang023@gmail.com.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
